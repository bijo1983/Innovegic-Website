FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an ERP implementation take?
A typical XentraERP implementation runs 3–9 months depending on modules, entities and data migration scope. We always start with a scoped pilot so you see working software within weeks — not at the end of the project.
Do you support multi-country payroll?
Yes. HumanVerse360 includes native payroll engines for the GCC (WPS-ready), India, the UK and the US. Statutory rules are maintained by our product team, so compliance updates arrive as part of the platform.
Can you work with our existing systems?
Absolutely. System integration is one of our core services — we connect our platforms with your existing ERP, HR, CRM and legacy systems through APIs, middleware and EDI so data flows without re-keying.
How do you approach AI governance and data privacy?
Every AI engagement includes a governance framework covering data privacy, access control, auditability and human oversight — aligned with your industry's regulatory environment and your organization's risk appetite.
Do you offer support after go-live?
Yes. Our managed services provide SLA-backed monitoring, application management and continuous optimization after every implementation, so systems keep improving long after launch.
