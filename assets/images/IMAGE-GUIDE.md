# Innovegic — Stock Image Sourcing & Placement Guide

Curated plan for premium, royalty-free photography (Unsplash / Pexels /
Pixabay — all free for commercial use, no attribution required).

**Drop each downloaded photo into this folder tree with the exact filename
below** — the site will be wired to these paths, so no code changes are
needed after the images land.

## Art direction rules (apply to every selection)

- Cool tones (blues/teals/neutrals) that harmonize with the brand palette;
  avoid warm orange office shots.
- Natural light, modern glass-and-wood offices; authentic working moments,
  not posed handshakes or people pointing at whiteboards.
- Real screens with real UI where visible; no obvious green-screen mockups.
- Diverse, professional teams in business or business-casual attire.
- Minimum 1600px wide originals; export at the "large" (≈1920px) size.
- Landscape orientation unless noted.

## Optimization checklist (done at integration time)

1. Resize to the target width, export JPEG quality ≈82, or convert to WebP.
2. Keep each file under ~250KB.
3. `loading="lazy"` + explicit width/height (already the site standard).
4. Update the og:image meta tags to the new hero/about/services images.
