Innovation, Intelligence & Technology — In Everything We Do

Innovegic is a digital transformation and enterprise technology company headquartered in the Kingdom of Bahrain, serving organizations across the GCC, India, the UK and the US.

Our Story

From IT Consultancy to a Product-Led Technology Company

Innovegic was founded on a simple conviction: organizations in our region deserve world-class technology built with deep local understanding. What began as a software consultancy has grown into a product-led enterprise technology company with a portfolio spanning ERP, human capital management, quality systems, document management, learning platforms and sports technology.

Today, our platforms power mission-critical operations for healthcare providers, government entities, manufacturers and enterprises — while our consultants guide leadership teams through AI adoption, cloud migration and end-to-end digital transformation.
