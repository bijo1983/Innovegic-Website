Cookie Policy

How we use cookies on www.innovegicit.com — and how you stay in control.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and understand how it is used. Similar technologies (such as local storage) work the same way, and this policy covers them too.

Cookies we use

Essential

Always active

Required for the website to function — for example, remembering your cookie preference itself (stored locally on your device as innovegic-cookie-consent). These cannot be switched off.

Analytics

Optional — only with your consent

Help us understand how visitors use the site — which pages are popular and how people find us — so we can improve it. Analytics tools are loaded only after you choose "Accept All". If you choose "Essential Only", no analytics cookies are set.

Managing your preferences

You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie Settings link in the footer of every page, which reopens the consent banner. You can also clear or block cookies through your browser settings — see your browser's help pages for instructions.

Questions?

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, contact us at info@innovegicit.com.
